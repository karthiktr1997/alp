What information do I need to provide for a consultation? +

To cast an accurate birth chart we need your date of birth, exact time of birth and place of birth. The more precise the birth time, the more detailed and accurate your reading will be.

Yes. Consultations are available both online (video / phone) and in person at our Chennai office. You can book whichever is most convenient — simply message us on WhatsApp to arrange a time.

I'm a complete beginner — can I still join a course? +

Absolutely. Our Basic ALP Astrology Training Course assumes no prior knowledge and starts from the very foundations. Many of our students began with zero background and now confidently read charts for family and friends.

What types of guidance can a reading cover? +

Personal birth-chart insights, career & financial growth, marriage & relationship compatibility, and health & life-path healing solutions. Every reading is tailored to the questions that matter most to you.

Are the remedies you suggest difficult to follow? +

Not at all. Our remedies are practical and respectful of your lifestyle — simple rituals, timing guidance and positive practices. We never use fear; we explain the why behind every suggestion.
